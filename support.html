Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → HeightMax Weekly → Cancel. Apple handles billing and cancellation for all in-app subscriptions.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open HeightMax, tap the paywall, and tap "Restore purchases". You must be signed in to the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

How do I request data deletion?

HeightMax does not require an account. The only identifier we hold is a random anonymous device ID stored on your phone. To delete your data:

  1. Find your anonymous ID in the app (Settings → About → Device ID).
  2. Email us at lachhman.anthony@gmail.com with that ID and the words "delete my data" in the subject.
  3. We will remove the quota rows and analytics events associated with that ID within 30 days.
