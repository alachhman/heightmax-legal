Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Keeper Weekly → Cancel. Apple handles billing and cancellation for all in-app subscriptions.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Keeper, open Settings → Plans & restore purchases, then tap “Restore”. You must be signed in to the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

What if an edit is interrupted?

Return to “Check my edit” in Keeper. Check the existing request before starting another. If it remains unresolved, contact support with your Ratings & editing support ID and the approximate time of the request.

How do I remove an edited photo from Keeper?

Open the edited result and choose “Remove the recovery copy.” This removes Keeper’s private recovery copy, not photos saved to your library or shared elsewhere. It does not erase all service records, remove provider records, cancel a subscription or return an edit to your allowance.

How do I request access to or deletion of my data?

For the new service account, open Keeper Settings → “Delete ratings & editing account” and confirm. Processing stops while removal is pending. The screen confirms the request, not completed deletion. Keep your support ID if you need help. If the response was not confirmed, use the retry control in Settings.

This removes the service identity, linked rating/job records and recovery copies. Your device photos, original-coaching records, separate billing/allowance records and provider records have a different scope. To request access or removal of those records, or if your version has no deletion control:

  1. Open Keeper Settings and copy your “Ratings & editing support” ID. For original coaching tools, also copy the anonymous device ID.
  2. Email lachhman.anthony@gmail.com with your request and the relevant ID. Do not send passwords.
  3. We may ask you to verify control of your session before releasing or deleting records. We will explain the scope of removal and any retained records.

Uninstalling Keeper does not delete server records or cancel your Apple subscription.
