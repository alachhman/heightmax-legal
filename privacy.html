Keeper — Privacy Policy

Release candidate prepared October 1, 2026. Not yet published.

Keeper, operated by Anthony Lachhman, helps you rate selected photos and make AI edits. This policy describes the new rating and editing services and the original photo-coaching tools.

What you send

You choose the photos to process. Before uploading, Keeper explains the AI processing and asks you to continue. Photo ratings and edit planning send selected photos through our Supabase backend to Google Gemini for visual observations. TypeSafe Jev receives those observations and your selected purpose or proposed change; it does not receive the photo files through this integration.

When you confirm an edit, Keeper sends the selected photo and edit instruction to Ideogram. The app labels the result as AI-edited. AI can change details you wanted to keep, so compare it with your original before saving or sharing.

Storage and removal

Downloaded working files and the latest result remain on your device. Unused working files older than 24 hours are eligible for cleanup when the new service screen opens; active files and pending work are protected. This is best-effort local cleanup, not a provider deletion deadline.

Use “Remove the recovery copy” on an edited result to request deletion of that result from Keeper’s private storage. Failed storage removals are retried by a scheduled worker. Previously issued download links may remain valid for up to five minutes. This does not remove copies saved to Photos, shared copies, provider records, billing records or all service records. It does not cancel a subscription or restore a used edit allowance.

In versions that offer the new services, Settings → “Delete ratings & editing account” requests removal of the authenticated service identity, its linked rating/job records and edited recovery copies. New processing is blocked after the request. A scheduled worker attempts removal after a 15-minute grace period and retries failed work. The confirmation means your request was accepted, not that deletion has finished. The saved request also prevents this installation from silently creating a replacement account.

This control does not delete device photos, copies you shared, original-coaching installation records, provider records, RevenueCat records, or separate purchase-allowance and aggregate-spending records. Contact support for requests concerning those records. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Keeper does not currently promise automatic deletion of all server records after a fixed period. Uninstalling does not delete backend records or cancel Apple subscriptions. Purchase-allowance records and aggregate spending records may remain separately from the service identity. Contact support to request access to or deletion of personal data; we will explain what can be removed and any records that must be retained.

Service providers

  • Google Gemini: selected photos and prompts for observations and the original coaching tools.
  • TypeSafe Jev: textual observations and decision context for ratings and proposed edits.
  • Ideogram: selected photo, edit instruction and generated output. Its API agreement excludes model training on inputs and outputs except content flagged for usage-policy violations.
  • Supabase: authentication, request processing, database records and private edited-photo storage.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: purchase processing, validation and restoration.
  • Sentry: optional crash and performance diagnostics when enabled.

Provider processing and retention follow the applicable provider agreements and account configuration. We do not promise zero retention or that every provider never uses data for model improvement. Providers may process data outside your country.

Your choices and requests

You can decline an AI upload, keep your original, remove an edited recovery copy, manage your photo-library permissions in iOS, and cancel a subscription in Apple Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your information.

For a privacy request, open Keeper Settings and copy the “Ratings & editing support” ID. Include the separate anonymous device ID if your request concerns the original coaching tools. Email lachhman.anthony@gmail.com. We may need to verify control of the relevant session; an ID by itself is not proof of ownership.

Security

Keeper uses encrypted network connections, authenticated service requests, restricted database access and private result storage. Download links are temporary and should not be shared with people you do not want to access the photo. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Age eligibility

The new AI services are intended for adults aged 18 or older. Do not submit photos depicting minors. Contact support if you believe a child’s information was submitted.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change. Privacy and support contact: lachhman.anthony@gmail.com.
