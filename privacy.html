Keeper — Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026.

This Privacy Policy describes how the Keeper mobile app ("Keeper", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares your information. It applies to the iOS app published under Apple ID com.antnee.heightmax.

1. Summary

  • Keeper does not require an account. A random anonymous device ID is generated and stored on your device. You can view and copy it in Settings → Your data → Anonymous device ID.
  • For analysis or enhancement, Keeper sends your selected photo and random ID to its Supabase backend. Analysis requests also include your Keeper Plus access status, and enhancement requests include the requested edit settings. The backend uses the ID for quota checks and sends the photo and prompt to Google Gemini; Google does not receive Keeper's ID or access status.
  • Keeper does not save uploaded photos in its product database. Google may retain submitted prompts and context, which can include your photo, and responses for up to 55 days for abuse monitoring. Authorized Google staff may review content flagged for policy violations.
  • We never sell your data, and we do not use any of this data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. We do not collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, or address.
  • Your Apple ID or other account credentials. Apple handles App Store purchases under its privacy policy; Keeper has no account sign-in.
  • Your contacts, calendar, microphone, location, or health data.
  • Advertising identifiers. Keeper does not use the iOS App Tracking Transparency framework because we do not track you across apps or websites.

5. Consent to AI analysis

Before Keeper sends a selected photo for AI processing, the app shows a disclosure sheet naming Google Gemini and explaining that the photo and random Keeper ID go to our backend. Analysis requests also include your Keeper Plus status; enhancement requests include edit settings. Google receives the photo and prompt, not the Keeper ID or access status. The sheet describes Google's possible retention of prompt context and responses for up to 55 days for abuse monitoring. You must tap "Agree and continue" before the photo is sent. Tapping "Not now" cancels the request. Your consent decision is stored locally.

You can decline the disclosure sheet at any time; this cancels the requested photo transfer. To request deletion of Keeper's server-side quota and analytics records, contact us as described in “Your rights.”

6. Children

Keeper is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app and you would like us to delete their anonymous ID, contact us.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR and the California CCPA), you may have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to certain processing. You can find and copy your random Keeper ID in Settings → Your data → Anonymous device ID. Include it with your deletion or access request so we can locate the related quota and analytics rows. We will respond within 30 days. This request covers Keeper's server-side quota and analytics records. Results and cached enhanced images in Keeper are removed when you clear app storage or uninstall the app; original photos in your photo library remain until you delete them there.

We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under the CCPA.

8. Security

Traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted with TLS. Photos are sent in HTTPS request bodies and are not saved in Keeper's product database. Google's Gemini API may retain prompts and context (including uploaded images) and responses for up to 55 days for abuse monitoring. Stored quota and analytics records are encrypted at rest by Supabase.

9. International transfers

Our backend runs on Supabase infrastructure in the United States. Google's Gemini API processes data on Google infrastructure that may include locations outside your country. By using Keeper, you consent to your data being transferred to these jurisdictions, subject to the protections described here.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the "Effective" date above and, for material changes, present an in-app notice the next time you open the app.
