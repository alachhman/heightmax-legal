HEIGHTMAX

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

HeightMax is built to be useful with as little personal data as possible. This page describes exactly what we collect, why, where it goes, and what we don't collect.

What we collect

  • An anonymous device ID generated on first launch and stored on your device. We use it to attach analysis events to a session and to enforce per-device monthly quotas. It's not tied to your identity.
  • The photos you submit for analysis are sent over HTTPS to our analysis service while a request is in flight. We do not store them after the response is returned. The AI model provider (Google Gemini) processes the photo to generate the analysis result and may temporarily process the request under their own terms.
  • Anonymous usage events such as "analysis started", "analysis completed", "paywall viewed", and "export completed". These contain no photo data and no personally identifying information.
  • Purchase receipts handled by Apple App Store or Google Play and routed through RevenueCat to verify entitlements.
  • Crash reports via Sentry when crash reporting is enabled in the build. These contain technical context (stack trace, app version, device model) but no photo data.

What we don't collect

  • Your name, email, phone, or any account profile.
  • Your contacts, calendar, or location.
  • Other photos in your library — only the photo you choose for an analysis.
  • Biometric measurements or claims about your real height.

Where data goes

  • Supabase hosts the analysis backend (edge functions and the per-device quota table). Photos are sent only for the duration of the analysis request.
  • Google Gemini performs the photo analysis. The photo is sent to Google's API to generate the response. Google's handling is governed by their AI service terms.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play handle all purchases. We never see your card details.
  • RevenueCat verifies subscription state.
  • Sentry receives crash reports if enabled.

Children

HeightMax is not intended for users under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has used the App, contact us and we'll help.

Your rights

You can stop sending data at any time by uninstalling the App. To request deletion of any analysis events or quota records associated with your anonymous ID, email lachhman.anthony@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be highlighted in the App.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email lachhman.anthony@gmail.com.
